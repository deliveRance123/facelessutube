How Deliverace Works

From raw script to a polished, high-definition video in under 60 seconds. No timeline scrubbing, keyframing, or manual subtitle syncing needed.

01

Import Your Visuals or Clips

Drag-and-drop animated clips from Meta AI (MP4, WebM, GIF) or static illustration scenes. Deliverace automatically detects resolution, loops short animations smoothly, or adds dynamic pan-and-zoom motion to still frames.

MP4 / WebM / Images
02

Paste or Generate the Script

Write your custom dialogue, storytelling lines, or paste a prepared voiceover script. Alternatively, type a concept and let the autonomous story engine draft a high-retention narrative.

Custom Script
03

Select Neural Voices or ElevenLabs

Choose from studio neural voices with natural inflection and pacing, or link your ElevenLabs API key to synthesize with your own voice clones and custom voice roster.

Studio / ElevenLabs
04

Pick Kinetic Caption Typography

Select high-impact animated caption styles: Gold Foil, Neon Cyan, TikTok Bold, Karaoke Glow, or Cinema Clean. Words highlight at millisecond precision matching the spoken audio.

Word-Level Timing
05

One-Click Render & Direct MP4 Export

Click Generate. The video engine composites the video clips, mixes clean speech, burns in subtitles, and delivers a download-ready MP4 file within seconds.
