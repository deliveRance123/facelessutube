Help & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about importing clips, voice synchronization, exports, and platform access.

Q: Can I import videos and animated clips created with Meta AI?

Yes! The Meta AI & Animated Clips Studio is specifically designed for this. You can upload MP4, WebM, MOV, or animated GIF clips generated from Meta AI or any external source. Paste your dialogue or narration script, pick a neural voice and caption style, and Deliverace will render a synchronized final video.

Q: How does ElevenLabs integration work?

Deliverace supports BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). In your dashboard settings or directly in the studio, enter your ElevenLabs API key. The platform immediately verifies your key and dynamically loads your custom cloned voices and library voices into your studio dropdown.

Q: Can I download my finished videos directly without watermarks?

Yes. Once your video is generated, it immediately appears in your studio player with an instant ⬇️ Download MP4 button. You own 100% of the exported files with no platform watermarks.

Q: What video formats and aspect ratios are supported?

Deliverace generates both Vertical Shorts (9:16) for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and social channels, and Widescreen Cinema (16:9) for horizontal film productions and cinematic displays.

Q: How do platform subscriptions and creator tiers work?

The platform operates with three tiers: Free, Pro, and Enterprise. Platform administrators have full console access to toggle between free open mode and subscription mode, as well as assign membership tiers to creators.
